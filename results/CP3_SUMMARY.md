# CP3 summary: new exploit types against the hardened judge

All numbers come from `results/cp3/cp3.json` (regenerated by `make reproduce` from `runs/cache` + `runs/embeddings`).
Pre-registration: plan amendment A7 + A7 addendum (written before any CP3 render was scored or any CP3 call was made).
Judges: J4H = the hardened VDA judge (the CP2 VDA rule is "classifier only", so J4H is the combined judge), J4 = CP1
classifier, Haiku 5.5 pairwise (pixels only, both orders): LLM-1 = per-call average (the CP2 "CP1 LLM" column), LLM-2 =
both-order vote (secondary). Lift = P(human loser wins | exploit on the loser, control on the winner) - P(... | control
on both); 95% cluster bootstrap over both items' app groups (2,000 resamples). "Gets through" = beyond noise in the +
direction (classifiers: CI lower bound > 0; Haiku: CI excludes 0 and clears the A/A band). 12 primary tests, no
multiplicity correction.

No human blind check was done (the author's decision). The new exploits are overlays on an unchanged design (X2 also rescales
it), each compared against a control with the same geometry.

## Which new types get through (plain)
- The hardened web-page judge (J4H) is fooled by a quality claim written into the page itself: it lets 6.7 percentage points
  more human losers win (+0.067 [+0.007, +0.157]), about two thirds of the CP1 probe's lift on the same pairs (+0.096), and its
  score moves 0.23 SD. It holds against the new frame style (the CP1 probe did not: +0.089), the award seal and the
  watermark.
- The CP1 probe is fooled by the laptop frame and by the hero claim. Training on the CP2 browser mockup carried over to a
  different frame style (score shift +0.058 -> -0.006), but training on strip captions did not carry over to text inside
  the design.
- Haiku pairwise: no new type beyond the pre-registered noise rule. The award seal comes closest (+0.078, CI excludes 0
  but its lower bound 0.008 is inside the A/A band 0.036).
- Watermark: no judge rewards it (all lifts <= 0).
- Caveats: 12 primary tests without multiplicity correction; the X3 result is a single type with a CI lower bound near
  0 (+0.007); geometry matching for X3/K3 holds the font, size, centre and colour but the two sentences differ by 10 px in
  width; no human blind check.
