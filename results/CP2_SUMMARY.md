# CP2 summary: hardened judge, before vs after

All numbers come from `results/cp2/cp2.json` (regenerated byte-identically by `make reproduce` from `runs/cache` +
`runs/embeddings`). Pre-registration: plan amendments A5 (design) and A6 (fixes after code review, written before any CP2
LLM judge call). Judges: Claude Haiku 5.5 everywhere; Claude Sonnet 5.5 only as the carry-over check.
Lift = P(human loser wins | edit on the loser) - P(... | matched control), both orders; 95% cluster-bootstrap CIs over
both items' groups. "Beyond noise" = CI excludes 0 and clears the A/A band (deterministic classifiers: CI excludes 0).

No human blind check was done (the author's decision). The note exploits (R2, R3, E4b) leave the design pixel-identical, and the
image exploits are overlays on an unchanged design compared against geometry-matched blank controls.

## What was built (all fit/chosen on build data only; tested on the 40 held-out VDA apps and GDE test templates)
- Note handling: (i) pixels only; (ii) extractive sanitizer (keeps only factual sentences; can't add text). Sanitizer
  generalization check on 8 unseen sentences: 6/6 adversarial dropped, 2/2 factual kept.
- J4H: the CP1 classifier on frozen UIClip embeddings, retrained with the build apps' overlay renders (E1, E2, E3, E4a,
  R4 + blank controls) labelled like their originals. E5 and format variants never used.
- J4crop (secondary): CP1 classifier on Haiku-located crops (crop applied to 94% of images).
- Combined judge: rule chosen on build pairs with out-of-fold classifier scores. VDA: "classifier only" (build agreement
  0.586 vs LLM 0.533; the stacked rule's 0.588 was within the 0.005 tie margin). GDE: "LLM decides, classifier breaks ties"
  (0.885 vs LLM 0.853, classifier 0.867).

Agreement on 100 VDA test pairs: Sonnet both-order 0.554 [0.504, 0.606] vs combined 0.583 [0.541, 0.627]. Because the VDA

## Honest reading
- What held: (1) dropping or sanitizing the designer's note removes the note-channel bias (rubric echo: +0.21 / +0.26 /
  +0.10 -> about 0); (2) training the classifier on overlay renders removes the overlay exploits it was trained on and the
  held-out E5 (new wording in a trained strip position) on web pages; (3) the combined judge is at least as accurate as
  the best CP1 arm on both datasets and order-invariant.
- What did not: (1) on graphic designs the combination rule keeps the LLM in charge, so its weakness to presentation polish
  and buzzword captions on close pairs carries straight through (mockup +0.19); (2) the augmented GDE classifier became
  exploitable by the held-out stamp (+0.24) and flattery (+0.23), even though flattery was in its training set; (3)
  both-order voting removes position dependence but, with one sample per order, cuts no exploit lift by itself (LLM-2 equals
  the per-call average on every pair).
- Caveats: E5 is new wording in a trained position, not a new exploit type; on VDA the sanitizer reduced the neutral control
  note to nothing, so arm (ii) equals arm (i) there except a one-sided neutral sentence on R3/E4b losers; on GDE it reduced
  R3/E4b to exactly the control note (A/A by construction); two VDA apps have byte-identical screenshots; COMB-ii and
  COMB-sonnet use rules chosen on Haiku pixels-only build votes; GDE crop boxes differ in width between edit and control.
